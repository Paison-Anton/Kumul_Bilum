# Kumul Bilum

Kumul Bilum is a static small-business website for a Papua New Guinea artisan brand. It showcases handmade bilums, shares stories about weaving traditions, and helps customers enquire about products and partnerships.

## Website Features

- Six bilum listings with locally stored photographs, short descriptions, and "Price on enquiry" labels.
- A product detail page for Umboldi Bilum 131214, including a nine-image gallery and an enquiry link.
- Story and services information about the business and its craft.
- A contact and registration form for product, gift, and partnership enquiries.
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile screens.

Prices for the six-image collection are shown as "Price on enquiry" until confirmed item prices are available.

## Pages

- `index.html`: home page and bilum collection.
- `product.html`: Umboldi Bilum 131214 details and photo gallery.
- `services.html`: business story and services.
- `contact.html`: contact details and enquiry form.
